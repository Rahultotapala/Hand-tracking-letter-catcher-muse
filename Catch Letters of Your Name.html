How to play

  • Move: wave your hand left and right in front of the camera. The catcher follows your index finger.
  • Catch in order: only the glowing letter counts next. It’s the one lit up above.
  • Watch out: catching a wrong letter, or letting your letter fall, costs a heart.
  • Finish your name for a bonus and a faster next round.
  • No camera? Move the mouse over the board, drag on touch screens, or use ← → keys.

Letter guide

A The next letter of your name — catch it!
